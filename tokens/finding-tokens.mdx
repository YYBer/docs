Every token listed on Fibe has a dedicated page that brings together everything you need to make an informed trading decision — live price, volume, market cap, interactive charts, a full orderbook, and a stream of recent trades. Whether you know exactly what you're looking for or you want to dig into an unfamiliar asset, the search bar and token detail pages put that information at your fingertips in seconds.

## Search for a token

You can reach any token on Fibe using three different identifiers:

- **Name** — type the full or partial name of the project (e.g., `Solana` or `Bonk`)
- **Ticker symbol** — enter the token's symbol (e.g., `SOL`, `BONK`, `JUP`)
- **Contract address** — paste the full on-chain mint address to find a token that may not yet be widely indexed by name

The search bar is always visible at the top of the Fibe interface. Results appear as you type, showing the token logo, name, ticker, and current price so you can confirm you have the right asset before clicking through.

<Tip>
  Always verify the contract address before trading an unfamiliar token. Scammers frequently create tokens with names and tickers identical to legitimate projects. On any token's detail page, Fibe displays the on-chain mint address — cross-reference it with the project's official website or a trusted source like Solscan before placing an order.
</Tip>

## Find a specific token step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search">
    Click the search bar at the top of any Fibe page, or press **/** on your keyboard to focus it instantly from anywhere in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your query">
    Enter the token name, ticker symbol, or full contract address. Fibe surfaces matching results in a dropdown list ranked by relevance and trading volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the token">
    Click the token in the results list. You land on its dedicated token detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the token data">
    Take a moment to review all available information before trading. The detail page shows current price, 24-hour change, volume, market cap, an interactive price chart, the live orderbook, and a feed of recent trades.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take action">
    When you're ready, use the trade panel on the right side of the token page to swap, place a limit order, or open a perpetual futures position directly from the same view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you see on a token detail page

Every token page on Fibe is packed with real-time data to support your research:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Price & 24h Change" icon="chart-line">
    The current market price alongside the absolute and percentage change over the last 24 hours, updated in real time via WebSocket.
  </Card>
  <Card title="24h Volume" icon="bars-staggered">
    Total trading volume in both token units and USD equivalent over the past 24 hours — a key indicator of liquidity and market interest.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Market Cap" icon="coins">
    Circulating supply multiplied by current price. Useful for gauging the relative size of an asset and comparing it to peers in the same sector.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interactive Chart" icon="chart-candlestick">
    Candlestick or line chart with selectable intervals from 1 minute to 1 month. Draw tools and technical indicators help you identify patterns and entry points.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Orderbook" icon="list-ol">
    Live bid and ask depth displayed as a traditional orderbook ladder. Bid/ask spread and cumulative depth are shown at a glance so you can assess liquidity before trading.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent Trades" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    A real-time feed of the most recent trades for the token — price, size, side (buy or sell), and timestamp. Helps you see where market participants are actively transacting.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Market cap data is derived from on-chain circulating supply figures. For newly launched tokens or those with complex vesting schedules, fully-diluted valuation (FDV) may differ significantly from circulating market cap. Both figures are shown on the token detail page where available.
</Note>
