The Explore section of Fibe is your starting point when you want to discover what's moving in the market rather than search for a specific token. It surfaces the most relevant tokens across a range of curated views — trending assets, fresh listings, highest trading volume, top gainers, and steepest losers — and lets you drill into any sector or theme so you can spot opportunities that align with your strategy.

## Filter and sort options

Explore gives you several pre-built views to slice the market the way you want:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trending" icon="fire">
    Tokens ranked by a composite signal of recent price action, volume acceleration, and on-chain activity. Updated every few minutes to reflect what the market is paying attention to right now.
  </Card>
  <Card title="New Listings" icon="sparkles">
    Recently listed tokens ordered by listing date. Use this view to catch early-stage assets before they gain wider attention. Pair with on-chain data from the token detail page to assess legitimacy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Top Volume" icon="arrow-trend-up">
    Tokens sorted by 24-hour trading volume in descending order. High volume indicates strong market participation and tighter spreads — useful when you need liquidity for larger positions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gainers" icon="arrow-up">
    The biggest percentage price increases over the last 24 hours. A quick way to spot momentum and breakout candidates, though always confirm with chart and volume data before acting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Losers" icon="arrow-down">
    The sharpest declines over the last 24 hours. Useful for identifying tokens that may be oversold or for monitoring assets you already hold that are under pressure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Categories & Themes" icon="tag">
    Browse tokens grouped by sector — DeFi, gaming, AI, memecoins, real-world assets, and more. Use this to build sector-based strategies or find alternatives within a theme you're already interested in.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Browse by category or theme

Fibe organises tokens into categories and thematic groups that reflect how the Solana ecosystem is structured. To browse by category:

1. Open **Explore** from the main navigation.
2. Select the **Categories** tab at the top of the Explore panel.
3. Click any category card — for example, **DeFi**, **Gaming**, or **AI** — to see all tokens in that group.
4. Sort the category list by price change, volume, or market cap to surface the most relevant tokens.

Categories are curated and updated regularly as new sectors emerge on Solana. If you're interested in a specific narrative or on-chain trend, the category view is the fastest way to see all the major players at once.

## Explore tokens step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Explore">
    Click **Explore** in the Fibe top navigation bar. The Explore page opens with the **Trending** view active by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a view or filter">
    Choose from **Trending**, **New Listings**, **Top Volume**, **Gainers**, or **Losers** using the tab bar at the top of the list. Each view re-sorts the token list instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a category filter (optional)">
    Click the **Category** dropdown and select a sector — for example, **Memecoins** or **Real-World Assets** — to narrow the list to tokens in that theme. Combine with any of the sort views for a targeted result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click into a token">
    Click any token row to open its full detail page, where you can review charts, orderbook depth, recent trades, and place a trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to watchlist">
    Click the star icon on any token row or detail page to add it to your personal watchlist. Your watchlist is accessible from the Portfolio section for quick monitoring.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use Explore alongside [Pulse](/tokens/pulse) for the sharpest market intelligence. Explore shows you the structured, category-level picture; Pulse surfaces real-time anomalies and signals — together, they give you both the macro view and the early-warning system.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Token rankings in the Trending and Gainers views refresh frequently throughout the day. If you notice a token move off a list quickly, check its chart directly — short-lived spikes can indicate low liquidity rather than sustained momentum.
</Note>
