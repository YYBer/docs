On every token detail page in Fibe, you'll find a **Similar Tokens** panel that surfaces a curated list of related assets. Whether you're looking to spread risk across a sector, find a cheaper-entry alternative to a token you like, or simply discover what else is moving in the same thematic space, Similar Tokens saves you the manual work of scanning the market yourself.

## How similarity is determined

Fibe's similarity engine evaluates tokens across three primary dimensions:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sector & Theme" icon="layer-group">
    Tokens that operate in the same category — DeFi protocols, gaming assets, AI-adjacent projects, memecoins, and so on — are treated as potential peers. Sector classification is based on on-chain function and project metadata.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Market Cap Range" icon="scale-balanced">
    Fibe weights similarity towards tokens with comparable market capitalisation. A micro-cap memecoin and a large-cap DeFi protocol may share a broad category but behave very differently — so market cap band is a key filter.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Correlated Price Action" icon="chart-mixed">
    Tokens whose price history shows correlated movement patterns over recent periods are surfaced as similar. Correlated assets often respond to the same macro or sector-specific catalysts, making them relevant comparisons.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

On-chain behaviour is also considered — for example, tokens that attract similar wallet demographics or have comparable liquidity profiles are more likely to be grouped together. The result is a similarity score that goes beyond surface-level categorisation.

## How to use Similar Tokens

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open any token detail page">
    Search for or navigate to a token you're interested in. You can use the search bar at the top of Fibe, or browse to a token through [Explore](/tokens/explore-tokens) or [Pulse](/tokens/pulse).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the Similar Tokens panel">
    On the token detail page, scroll down past the chart and orderbook sections. The **Similar Tokens** panel appears below the main trading data, showing up to 10 suggested tokens with their current price, 24-hour change, and volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare tokens side by side">
    Click any token in the Similar Tokens panel to open its detail page in the same view. Use your browser's back navigation to return to the original token and continue comparing across the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify your target">
    Once you've reviewed the similar tokens and found one that fits your strategy, use the trade panel on its detail page to swap, place a limit order, or open a perpetual futures position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use cases

<Accordion title="Portfolio diversification">
  If you're already holding a significant position in one token and want exposure to the same sector without concentrating risk further, Similar Tokens surfaces the most relevant alternatives in the same market cap range. Spreading across two or three correlated assets can reduce single-token risk while keeping you exposed to the sector thesis you believe in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Finding lower-cap alternatives">
  Found a large-cap token whose upside feels limited? Similar Tokens can surface smaller-cap peers in the same sector that haven't yet moved as much. These carry higher risk, but they can offer asymmetric upside if the sector thesis plays out.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Discovering emerging tokens in a theme">
  When a sector starts to heat up — for example, AI tokens gaining momentum — Similar Tokens on one of the leading assets in that sector will surface other tokens in the same thematic group, including newer or less well-known projects that may not yet appear in the Trending or Top Volume lists.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stress-testing a thesis">
  If you're considering a trade based on a sector-level view, reviewing similar tokens helps you validate whether the opportunity is specific to one token or broader across the category. If only one token is moving while its peers are flat, that's worth investigating before you commit.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Use Similar Tokens alongside [Explore](/tokens/explore-tokens) for a complete sector-based strategy workflow. Start with Explore's category view to identify a sector you want exposure to, then use Similar Tokens on your leading candidate to find the best-fit assets within that space — giving you both the macro picture and the granular comparison you need to make a confident decision.
</Tip>
