Fibe gives you two straightforward paths to get crypto into your wallet: buy directly with fiat currency through a trusted on-ramp provider, or deposit crypto you already own and put it to work through swaps or trades. Both routes land tokens in your connected Solana wallet and have you ready to trade within minutes.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="With Fiat">
    Buying with fiat lets you go from dollars (or your local currency) to on-chain tokens without leaving Fibe. Fibe partners with leading on-ramp providers to process card and bank payments securely.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Buy panel">
        From any page in Fibe, click **Buy** in the top navigation bar. The buy panel slides open on the right side of your screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your token">
        Search for or select the token you want to purchase. You can search by name, ticker symbol, or contract address. Fibe shows the current price and a preview of how many tokens your fiat amount will buy.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your fiat amount">
        Type the amount you want to spend in your local currency. The panel updates in real time to show the estimated token amount you will receive after provider fees.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select a payment method">
        Choose from the available payment methods presented by the on-ramp provider:

        - **Credit or debit card** — Visa and Mastercard are accepted by most providers. Fastest settlement, typically under 5 minutes.
        - **Bank transfer (ACH / SEPA)** — Lower fees than card payments. Settlement takes 1–3 business days depending on your region and provider.
        - **Apple Pay / Google Pay** — Available where supported by the active provider.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete the on-ramp flow">
        Fibe hands off to your selected on-ramp provider. Complete identity verification if prompted (first-time buyers may need to submit a government-issued ID), then confirm payment. You remain in a secure provider-hosted flow throughout.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Receive tokens in your wallet">
        Once the provider confirms payment, tokens are sent directly to your connected Solana wallet. You will see your balance update in the Fibe portfolio view. Most card purchases settle within 5–10 minutes.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    ### Supported on-ramp providers

    Fibe partners with leading on-ramp providers including **MoonPay** and **Transak**. The provider available to you depends on your country of residence. Fibe automatically surfaces the best option for your region when you open the Buy panel.

    <Note>
      On-ramp fees — including processing and network fees — are set entirely by the provider, not by Fibe. Always review the fee breakdown shown in the provider's confirmation screen before you submit payment.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="With Crypto">
    If you already hold crypto on another wallet or exchange, the fastest way to start trading on Fibe is to deposit it directly. No on-ramp provider is involved, so there are no fiat processing fees or identity checks beyond what you've already completed.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect or create your Solana wallet">
        Fibe works with any Solana-compatible wallet such as Phantom, Backpack, or Solflare. Click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right corner and approve the connection request in your wallet extension.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find your deposit address">
        Navigate to **Portfolio → Deposit**. Fibe displays your wallet's public address and a QR code. This is the address you send crypto to.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send crypto from your source">
        In your external wallet or exchange, initiate a withdrawal to your Fibe wallet address. Double-check the network — Fibe operates on **Solana (SOL)**. Sending assets on the wrong network will result in permanent loss.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
        Solana blocks confirm in under a second. Your deposited tokens appear in your Fibe portfolio within moments of the transaction landing on-chain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Swap or trade">
        With tokens in your wallet, you can immediately swap into any other supported token, place limit or market orders, or open perpetual futures positions — all without leaving Fibe.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Always verify you are sending on the **Solana network**. Deposits sent on Ethereum, BSC, or other EVM-compatible networks cannot be recovered by Fibe.
    </Warning>

    <Tip>
      If you hold tokens on a Solana-native DEX aggregator, you can bridge or swap them before depositing, or simply connect the same wallet you already use — your existing balances are immediately visible in Fibe.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
