Fibe's referral program lets you earn rewards simply by introducing friends to the platform. Every time someone signs up using your unique referral link and begins trading, both of you receive rewards — creating an ongoing incentive that grows as your network does. The more active traders you refer, the more you earn, and referral rewards stack directly on top of Fibe's broader points system so your earnings compound over time.

## Find Your Referral Link

Your personal referral link is generated automatically when you connect your wallet to Fibe. To locate it:

1. Click your wallet address or profile icon in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.
2. Select **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu.
3. Navigate to the **Referrals** tab.
4. Copy your unique referral link — it looks like `https://fibe.com?ref=YOUR_CODE`.

<Note>
  Your referral code is permanently tied to your wallet address. It remains valid indefinitely and works across all Fibe features including spot trading, perpetuals, and swaps.
</Note>

## How Referral Rewards Work

When a friend uses your link, Fibe tracks the relationship between your wallet and theirs. Rewards are distributed based on their ongoing trading activity — not just the initial sign-up — so you continue earning as long as your referred friends remain active.

- **You earn** a percentage of the trading fees generated by every wallet you referred, credited directly to your account.
- **Your friend earns** a fee discount or bonus on their first trades, giving them an immediate benefit for joining through your link.
- **Both accounts** receive a one-time sign-up bonus in points when the referred wallet completes its first trade.

Rewards are calculated and settled on a rolling basis. You can track your referral earnings and the activity of your referred wallets from the **Referrals** tab in your Account Settings at any time.

## Share Your Link and Claim Rewards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your referral link">
    Go to **Account Settings → Referrals** and click the **Copy Link** button next to your unique referral URL. You can also copy just the referral code if you want to append it to a custom URL or share it in a message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with your network">
    Send your link to friends via direct message, post it on social media, or include it in content you create about Fibe. Anyone who clicks the link and connects their wallet will be tracked as your referral automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your friend signs up and trades">
    Once your friend connects their wallet through your link and executes their first trade, the referral relationship is confirmed on-chain. Both of you receive the sign-up bonus in points immediately after that first trade settles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor your referral earnings">
    Return to **Account Settings → Referrals** to see a live breakdown of your referred wallets, their trading volume, and the cumulative rewards you've earned. Your fee-share rewards accumulate in real time as your referred friends trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your rewards">
    Referral rewards are credited to your Fibe account balance automatically. Navigate to the **Rewards** section of your dashboard to view your available balance and initiate a withdrawal to your connected wallet at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing Best Practices

Make the most of your referral link by sharing it in the right places:

- **Twitter / X** — Post about a trade you made on Fibe and include your referral link. Wallets that interact with your post on-chain are automatically tracked if they sign up through your link.
- **Telegram and Discord** — Crypto communities on these platforms are often looking for new tools. A brief explanation of Fibe's key features alongside your link performs well in relevant groups.
- **Content and tutorials** — If you create YouTube videos, blog posts, or written guides about Solana trading, embed your referral link in the content. Long-form content continues generating sign-ups long after it's published.

<Tip>
  Referral rewards and Fibe's point system stack on top of each other. Every referred friend who trades contributes to your points balance in addition to fee-share rewards. Focus on referring active traders rather than maximizing raw sign-up count — a smaller group of high-volume traders will earn you far more than a large group of inactive wallets. See the [Rewards & Points](/getting-started/rewards-and-points) page for full details on how the two systems combine.
</Tip>
