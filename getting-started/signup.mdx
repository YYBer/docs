Getting started with Fibe takes only a few minutes. Because Fibe is a non-custodial platform built on Solana, your "account" is your wallet — there is no username or password to create. Simply connect a Solana-compatible wallet, and you're ready to trade, track positions, and explore the full platform.

## Connect Your Wallet and Start Trading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit fibe.com">
    Open your browser and navigate to [fibe.com](https://fibe.com). The landing page gives you an overview of the platform and a prominent **Launch App** button that takes you straight into the trading interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Connect Wallet'">
    Inside the app, click the **Connect Wallet** button in the top-right corner of the navigation bar. A wallet selection modal will appear listing all supported Solana wallets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and connect your Solana wallet">
    Choose your preferred wallet from the list. Fibe supports all major Solana wallets — see the [Wallet Compatibility](#wallet-compatibility) section below for the full list. Your wallet extension or mobile app will open and prompt you to approve the connection request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection request">
    Review the connection prompt in your wallet. Fibe only requests read access to your public key at this stage — no funds are moved and no transaction is signed during the initial connection. Tap or click **Connect** (or **Approve**) to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your dashboard">
    Once connected, your wallet address appears in the navigation bar and your portfolio dashboard loads automatically. From here you can swap tokens, view your balances, place orders, track other wallets, and access all platform features.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Fibe is entirely non-custodial. Your private keys never leave your wallet — Fibe can only see your public address and request your signature for on-chain transactions. You remain in full control of your funds at all times.
</Note>

## Wallet Compatibility

Fibe supports all wallets that implement the Solana Wallet Standard. The following wallets are fully tested and recommended:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Phantom" icon="ghost" href="https://phantom.app">
    The most widely used Solana wallet. Available as a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, Brave, and Edge, plus iOS and Android mobile apps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Backpack" icon="backpack" href="https://backpack.app">
    A next-generation wallet with built-in xNFT support and a clean interface. Available as a browser extension and mobile app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Solflare" icon="sun" href="https://solflare.com">
    A feature-rich wallet with hardware wallet support (Ledger) via USB or Bluetooth. Available as a browser extension and mobile app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Other Solana Wallets" icon="wallet">
    Any wallet that supports the Solana Wallet Standard — including Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet, and Ledger (via a companion extension) — will work seamlessly with Fibe.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If you're using a hardware wallet like a Ledger device, connect it through Solflare or Phantom's Ledger integration. This gives you the security of cold storage while still being able to sign trades on Fibe.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Do I need to create a username or password?">
  No. Fibe uses your Solana wallet as your identity. There is no separate username, password, or email address required. Your wallet's public key serves as your unique account identifier on the platform.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a KYC or identity verification process?">
  Fibe is a permissionless decentralized exchange. There is no KYC process required to connect your wallet and start trading. Depending on your jurisdiction, you are responsible for complying with any applicable local regulations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I connect multiple wallets?">
  Yes. Fibe supports multi-wallet portfolio management. After your initial connection you can add additional wallets through the Portfolio section to track all your holdings in one consolidated view.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I do if my wallet isn't listed?">
  If your wallet supports the Solana Wallet Standard, it should appear automatically in the connection modal. If it does not appear, make sure your wallet extension is installed and unlocked in your browser, then refresh the page and try again.
</Accordion>
