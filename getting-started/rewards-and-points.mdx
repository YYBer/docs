Fibe runs two complementary earning programs side by side — a **Reward System** that pays out based on trading activity, referrals, and milestone achievements, and a **Point System** that tracks your overall platform engagement and unlocks exclusive benefits. Understanding how both work — and how they interact — lets you extract significantly more value from every trade and referral you make on the platform.

---

## Reward System

The Reward System is Fibe's direct cash-equivalent earnings layer. Rewards are denominated in tokens and credited to your Fibe account based on specific on-chain and platform actions.

### What Triggers Rewards

**Trading Activity**
A portion of the protocol's fee revenue is redistributed to active traders. The more volume you generate across spot trading, limit orders, and perpetuals, the larger your share of the reward pool. Rewards scale with both the size and frequency of your trades, incentivizing consistent engagement over one-off large trades.

**Referrals**
When a wallet you referred executes trades on Fibe, you earn an ongoing fee-share reward tied to their activity. This reward continues indefinitely as long as the referred wallet remains active — meaning a strong referral network can generate passive rewards even during periods when you're not trading yourself.

**Milestones**
Fibe issues one-time milestone rewards when you reach defined thresholds on the platform. Examples include completing your first swap, reaching cumulative trading volume targets, maintaining consecutive active trading days, and becoming a top-tier referrer. Milestone rewards are designed to recognize long-term commitment to the platform rather than short-term activity spikes.

### Claiming Rewards

Rewards accumulate in your Fibe account in real time. Navigate to the **Rewards** section of your dashboard to see your pending and claimable balance. Claimable rewards can be withdrawn to your connected Solana wallet at any time — there is no lock-up period for earned rewards.

<Note>
  Reward percentages and pool allocations may change over time as Fibe adjusts its fee structure and growth programs. Check the Rewards dashboard for current rates applicable to your tier.
</Note>

## Point System

The Point System tracks your cumulative engagement with Fibe and serves as the backbone of the platform's loyalty program. Points do not expire and accumulate across every interaction you have with the platform.

### How Points Are Earned

| Activity | Points Awarded |
|---|---|
| Completing your first trade | Bonus points one-time |
| Every swap or market order | Points per trade volume |
| Every limit order placed | Points per order |
| Trading perpetuals | Points per contract volume |
| Referring a new wallet | Points when referred wallet's first trade settles |
| Referred wallet's ongoing trades | Percentage of their points allocation |
| Daily platform login | Small daily streak bonus |
| Completing milestone challenges | Bonus point rewards |
| Participating in platform events | Event-specific point multipliers |

### What Points Can Be Used For

Points accumulated on Fibe unlock tangible benefits across the platform:

- **Fee tier upgrades** — Higher point balances move you into lower-fee trading tiers, reducing the cost of every trade you place.
- **Exclusive features** — Certain advanced trading tools, analytics dashboards, and early-access features are gated by point thresholds.
- **Reward multipliers** — Maintaining a high point balance boosts the rate at which you accumulate future points and rewards, compounding your earnings over time.
- **Token redemption** — Points can be redeemed for platform tokens or other incentives during designated redemption windows announced on the Fibe dashboard.
- **Leaderboard rankings** — Your point total determines your standing on Fibe's public leaderboard, which feeds into additional prize pools and recognition programs run periodically by the platform.

<Tip>
  The single most effective strategy for maximizing total earnings on Fibe is to combine all three earning streams simultaneously. Trade actively to earn volume-based rewards and points. Refer high-volume traders to generate passive fee-share rewards and a multiplier on their points allocation. Hit milestones systematically by working through the milestone list in your dashboard — many milestones reward actions you would already be taking as an active trader. This three-way compounding effect means your earnings grow non-linearly as you engage more deeply with the platform.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="How do I check my points balance?">
  Your current points balance is always visible in the top navigation bar next to your wallet address once you're connected. For a full breakdown — including points earned per activity category, your tier status, and your position on the leaderboard — navigate to **Account Settings → Points & Rewards**. The dashboard shows a real-time running total and a history of every point-earning event tied to your wallet.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do points expire?">
  No. Points on Fibe do not have an expiry date. Every point you earn is permanently credited to your wallet's account and will remain there regardless of how long you are inactive. That said, certain time-limited events or seasonal multipliers do have expiry dates on the multiplier itself — the base points earned during those events are kept even after the event ends.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I combine referral rewards and trading rewards?">
  Yes — and this combination is specifically how the platform is designed to be used. Referral rewards (fee-share from referred wallets' trades) and trading rewards (your own trading volume rewards) are calculated independently and credited to the same Fibe account balance. They do not offset each other. Similarly, points earned from referrals and points earned from your own trading both accumulate in the same points total. There is no cap on how many earning streams can be active simultaneously.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do reward tiers work?">
  Fibe uses a tiered structure where your trading volume over a rolling 30-day window determines your current tier. Higher tiers receive a larger percentage of the fee-share reward pool and earn points at an accelerated rate. Your tier is recalculated automatically each day based on the previous 30 days of on-chain activity. You can see your current tier and the thresholds for the next tier in the **Rewards** section of your dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When are rewards paid out?">
  Rewards accumulate continuously as on-chain activity occurs. They become available to claim in near real-time — typically within a few minutes of the triggering trade settling on Solana. There is no weekly or monthly payout cycle; you can withdraw your available reward balance to your wallet at any time from the **Rewards** dashboard.
</Accordion>
