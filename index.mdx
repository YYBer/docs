## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [fibe.com](https://fibe.com) and connect your Solana wallet to unlock the full platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your account">
    Deposit SOL or any supported token directly to your Fibe wallet from any Solana address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start trading">
    Swap tokens instantly, set limit orders, or explore perpetuals — all from a single dashboard.
